title: Authorization
description: Aconite's offline keyfile authorization — what's gated, what never is, and how to activate.
---

Aconite uses a simple, offline authorization. There is no always-on connection,
no login required at play time, and no phoning home while you work.

## Playback is never gated

The most important thing to know: **playing Aconite is never gated.** An
unauthorized instance still makes sound. You can load a project, hear every patch,
and perform with it. Authorization only unlocks **authoring** — saving your own
work. Your music and your sessions are never held hostage.

## The offline keyfile

Activation is a **keyfile** you receive when you buy Aconite. Authorizing is a
one-time, offline step:

1. Get your keyfile from your account or purchase email.
2. In Aconite, open authorization from the header menu.
3. Either **drag and drop** the keyfile onto the window, or point Aconite at its
   **file path**.

That is it. No internet connection is needed for this step, so it works on an
offline studio machine.

:::note
Because activation is a file, you can authorize a machine that never touches the
internet: copy the keyfile over, drop it in, done.
:::

## After you authorize

Once authorized, authoring unlocks: you can save user presets, save your themes,
and keep your edits. Everything you could hear before, you can now also keep.

## Moving to a new machine

Keep your keyfile somewhere safe (the same place you back up your presets and
themes is a good choice). To set up Aconite on another computer, install the
plugin and drop the same keyfile in.

If you run into any trouble activating, see
[Troubleshooting & FAQ](/aconite-manual/reference/troubleshooting/).
