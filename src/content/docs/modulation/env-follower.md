---
title: Envelope follower
description: The per-voice envelope follower — a source that tracks the signal's level, the classic auto-wah ingredient.
---

The **envelope follower** is a modulation source that tracks the signal's own
level per voice. Route it to cutoff and you have an auto-wah; route it anywhere and
a patch responds dynamically to how hard you play.
