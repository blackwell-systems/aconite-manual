---
title: The modulation matrix
description: The per-voice and bus modulation matrices — Source, Via, Destination, Depth — plus the right-click Modulate workflow and macros.
---

This is the single most important chapter for "how do I make it move." Aconite has
an 8-slot per-voice **modulation matrix** and a **bus matrix** over the effects and
master. Each slot routes a **Source** through an optional **Via** transform
(Invert / Rectify / Quantize / Lag) to a **Destination** at a set **Depth**. You
can also set up modulation directly by right-clicking any control and choosing
**Modulate**, watch live modulation rings on the knobs, and drive destinations from
macros, MPE Press/Slide, MIDI CC, and curve lanes.
