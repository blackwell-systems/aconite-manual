Aconite trades CPU for fidelity, on purpose (see
[The Aconite philosophy](/aconite-manual/getting-started/philosophy/)). This
chapter is the practical guide to spending those cycles well: the oversampling
**Quality** tiers (1× / 2× / 4× / 8×), **Auto-HQ on bounce** so you can monitor
light and render pristine, **voice count**, and an ordered checklist of what to
turn down first when you need headroom.
