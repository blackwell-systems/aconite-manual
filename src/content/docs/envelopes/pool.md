---
title: The envelope pool
description: Six routable envelopes, their voicing models, ADSR plus velocity and curve, and the Cycle and Restart behaviors.
---

Aconite gives you a pool of **six routable envelopes** (Amp, Filter, and Env 3–6).
Each has a voicing model (ADS, Analogue, Digital, Vintage, Classic), full A/D/S/R
with Velocity, Quantize, and Curve, keyboard-follow, a **Cycle** mode
(Normal / Loop / One-shot) that turns an envelope into a repeating source, and a
**Restart** setting (From Current / From Zero) that decides its starting level.
