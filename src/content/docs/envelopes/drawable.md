---
title: Drawable envelopes
description: Draw mode — hand-drawn multi-segment envelope contours with sustain and loop markers.
---

Any envelope can swap its ADSR for a **drawn contour**. In Draw mode you shape a
hand-drawn, multi-segment curve with sustain and loop markers, per-segment
interpolation, and a live playhead — for envelopes that do exactly what you draw
rather than what four knobs allow.
