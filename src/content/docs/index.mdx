## What Aconite is

Aconite is an analog-modeling polysynth built by **Alkaloid Audio**. It sounds
like hardware because it models hardware: analog-modeled filters and oscillators,
per-voice character and drift, and a signal path you can reroute rather than a
fixed chain.

It leads with its **filters**. Two fully independent filters, each with its own
model drawn from a menu of seven, sit at the center of the instrument, and almost
everything else in the synth exists to feed them and move them.

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Filters, front and center" icon="setting">

    A per-voice modulation matrix, bus modulation over the effects and master,

    sequencer that double as modulation sources.
  </Card>
  <Card title="A full effects rack" icon="puzzle">
    Twelve effects — chorus, phaser, delay, multi-tap, reverb, tape, distortion,
    bit-crusher, compressor, transient shaper, EQ, and stereo imager — freely
    re-orderable, each insert or send/return.
  </Card>

## Who it's for

If you reach for a synth to sculpt a sound rather than pick one off a shelf,
Aconite is built for you. It rewards exploration: the character controls
interact, the filters have "nerd knobs" worth learning, and the modulation
system is deep enough to make a patch breathe. It is equally happy loading a
factory patch and playing.

## Formats

Aconite ships as **VST3**, **AU**, and a **Standalone** app, for **macOS** and
**Windows**.
